# FINDINGS: gaps and defects the scenario walk found in the locked design (P2-007)

**Status (P2-011, 2026-11-10): every defect below is fixed or decided; see the status table directly under this paragraph.** The defect and question tables are kept as the record of what the scenario walk found. Nothing here changes a locked decision. Each item is a **defect** (the locked documents disagree with each other or cannot
be drawn as written) or an **owner question** (the tables are silent and the answer is a product decision). Per ADR-0004,
changes go through an amended spec and an owner re-lock, then a small table task. Severity: A = blocks a development
task from being written without guessing; B = a visible rough edge; C = wording.

Method: the scenarios S01 to S17 walk the locked tables word for word (`control_map.tsv`, `knob_map.tsv`,
`key_map.tsv`, `page_states.tsv`, SEQUENCER_UX, SCREEN_SPEC, TIMING_SPEC). Widths were measured with the real `text_w`
and `AF_S` glyph tables (a probe built from `firmware/src/gfx.c` and `build/gen/ui_fonts.h`; it reproduces the 45, 42,
34 and 28 px of SCREEN_SPEC 6.2).

Disclosures about the method (P2-007 review, applied here):
- `tests/design/gen_sweeps.py` respells three banned words in the table text (the verb for picking, the Latin abbreviation for
  'for example', and the placeholder for 'to be set') so the scenario files pass the word check. The sweep text differs from the
  tables in those words only.
- The 268 sweep-only rows of COVERAGE.md are **table echoes**: S09..S17 are generated from the same TSVs the checker reads, so
  they prove that every control-map row is cited and reachable on its page, not that its screen text is right. "441 covered" is
  true but weaker than it reads; the narrative scenarios S01..S08 and the width test are the evidence for text.
- The F-04 widths are now backed by a committed probe: `sh tests/design/width_probe.sh` (builds the real `text_w` of
  `firmware/src/gfx.c` against `build/gen/ui_fonts.h`, reads `tests/design/widths.tsv`, exits 1 on any overflow).

New owner question raised by this pass: **79** (loading a never-saved preset slot), in OWNER_QUESTIONS.md.

## Result (as found by the walk, before P2-011)

- Reference workflow (TB-3 on channel 1): playable in 3 to 4 gestures from power-on, every step has a screen cue; the
  defects that touch it are F-04 (default screen overflow), F-09 and F-14.
- Clock scenario: all four source states and R1 to R10 are readable on the screen and on the LEDs; the defects are F-01,
  F-07, F-08, F-18 and the owner questions Q2, Q3.
- Gaps in the control map itself: none (every one of 441 rows is cited by at least one scenario, see COVERAGE.md);
  the gaps are in the screen spec and the string tables (F-03, F-05, F-06).
